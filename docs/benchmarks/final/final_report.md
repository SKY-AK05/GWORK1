## Methodology & Confidence

### Methodology

Research used four fetched sources produced during the run: two pages from the ORCHVATE website (OurInsights.html and Our-Journey.html) and two social-media handles (Facebook, Instagram) [1][2][3][4]. Search backends and methods included direct site fetches of orchvate.com pages, following site-provided social links, and automated registry-check attempts; the automated Companies House check failed because the research environment lacked a configured COMPANIES_HOUSE_API_KEY, producing an 'inconclusive_verification' status. Sources retrieved are dated around the run time (captures retrieved 2026-10-09 in the research metadata); however, explicit publication dates were not present on the fetched pages. Notable gaps: no Companies House or other UK registry documents were obtained, social pages required login for full content, the site root fetch experienced a backend/no-content failure during one attempt, and offline records (invoices/contracts) were not available. Total distinct external sources referenced in the report: 4 (two website pages, two social handles).

### Overall Confidence

Brand-level conclusions (that ORCHVATE operates a website, presents services, lists contact emails/phones, and maintains social handles) are well-supported (High confidence) because they are directly observable on the fetched pages [1][2][3][4]. Conclusions about UK legal registration are tentative (Medium-to-Low confidence) because no Companies House record or UK incorporation details were found in the fetched materials and the automated registry check could not complete due to environment/API limitations. What would change the assessment: provision of a Companies House registration number, certificate of incorporation, UK registered office, or audited third-party business reference would move the legal-entity question to High confidence; conversely, discovery of a clear India-incorporated legal entity or invoices showing non-UK billing would strengthen the assessment that no UK legal entity exists.
